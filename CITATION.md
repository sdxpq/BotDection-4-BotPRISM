# Citation

Thank you for building on our work. If you publish research results obtained
using or adapting code released in this repository, please cite the associated
paper in accordance with the [LICENSE](LICENSE).

## Current citation

Complete bibliographic details for the associated paper have not yet been
added to this repository. Until they are available here, please cite the
repository and identify the commit or release used in your experiments. Include
your actual access date in the format required by your venue.

Suggested reference:

> BotDection-4-BotPRISM contributors. *BotDection-4-BotPRISM* [Software repository].
> GitHub. https://github.com/sdxpq/BotDection-4-BotPRISM

```bibtex
@misc{botprism_repository,
  author       = {{BotDection-4-BotPRISM contributors}},
  title        = {{BotDection-4-BotPRISM}},
  howpublished = {GitHub repository},
  url          = {https://github.com/sdxpq/BotDection-4-BotPRISM}
}
```

This is a repository reference, not a reference to a published paper. It does
not assert a paper title, author list, venue, publication year, or DOI. When a
release supplies the paper's complete reference, please follow the citation
instructions in that release.

## Ideas and acknowledgments

If our work informs your research without your using the code, we also welcome

## Support and questions

If you find the repository useful, a Star would mean a lot to us. Starring is
entirely optional and does not replace a scholarly citation where one is
required. We are glad that our work can support your research, and we sincerely
appreciate your interest and support.

For questions, please open an issue or contact the first author identified in
the associated manuscript directly. Do not post confidential information in
public issues.
